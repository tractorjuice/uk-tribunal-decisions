About this database

This database contains 18,927 decisions from the First-tier Tribunal (Property Chamber) in England and the Residential Property Tribunal Wales, spanning 2001 to 2026. England decisions are sourced from GOV.UK and Wales decisions from residentialpropertytribunal.gov.wales.

  • Housing Act 2004 And Housing And Planning Act 2016 — 2,792 decisions

  • Park Homes — 744 decisions

Coverage

Tribunal regions: London, Chichester (South East), Manchester (North West), Birmingham (Midlands), Cambridge (East), Wales, Havant (Southern), Unknown, NS, VG, TR, NT, GB, National, RC.

Each record includes the decision date, case reference, property address, category, and a link to the full decision. Structured fields are extracted from the decision text: applicant (95%), respondent (95%), presiding judge (83%), outcome (54%).
